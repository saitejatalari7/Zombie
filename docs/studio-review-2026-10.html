Pre-launch studio review · Godot 4.6 · Android · 27 Oct 2026

Protocol: Crimson

A portrait zombie extraction shooter: burn the hives, run the contract, hold the LZ until the chopper lands. This review covers game quality, retention, monetization and what Google Play requires before release.

4.2/10

Verdict: not ready for soft launch. Greenlight a 2–3 week readiness sprint that ends in a Google Play closed test. A new personal developer account needs that closed test anyway: at least 12 testers for 14 days.

The core loop, the data-driven contracts, crash-safe saves and a distinctive white-and-crimson look are real strengths. What's missing is a tutorial, anything that brings players back, monetization, analytics and a build Play will accept. Combat swings between trivial and deadly.

Launch blockers

  1. No tutorial or first-time flow verified

    Nothing explains the controls, and the first thing a new player meets is a hive fight. This is the largest risk to day-1 retention. The has_completed_tutorial flag in game_manager.gd is never set to true.

  2. Combat swings between trivial and deadly measured

    In 60 seconds standing still, the player took 0 damage in s1_beacon, 40 in s2_medic and 1,104 in s4_core. Fighting the hives in s1_beacon cost 2,995 HP over 183 s, against 100 max HP. Causes:

    • Zombies have 6× health.
    • Packs spawn 16–22 m away and walk into a 7.5 m auto-fire ring.
    • Hive defenders ignore the global spawn cap.

  3. The build can't go to Play verified

    • It's an APK, not an AAB, and the Gradle build is off.
    • It's signed with the public test key. If that key signs the first upload, it becomes the app's permanent upload key, so it must never be used for that.
    • Target API is 35; Play now asks for 36.
    • The version code is fixed at 1.

  4. Back gesture quits mid-run; no pause on interruptions found in code

    Nothing handles the Android back request, and losing focus (an incoming call or the notification shade) doesn't pause the game. Loot not yet banked from the run is lost.

  5. No crash reporting, analytics or privacy policy verified

    Without them a soft launch can't be measured and Play's Data safety form can't be completed. Play flags apps above a 1.09% crash rate or 0.47% ANR rate.

Also confirmed:

  • Intel research cores are lost for good if you die after picking one up.
  • The hub teases "2 more weapons coming" that don't exist yet.
  • The icon has baked-in corners and no adaptive layers.
  • There's no cloud save.
  • About 6 MB of test files ship in the APK.
  • The QA bots write to the real save file.

Retention plan

Each feature builds on systems the game already has. Contracts are data tables, so most daily and event content is configuration, not new code.

  1. Guided first contract 4–6 days

    A scripted 3-minute sector-1 run built from the existing contract steps: one small hive, a molotov, a rocket "blast" and a short evac. It ends on the existing "Upgrade now" button.

  2. Daily Ops ~1 week

    Three seeded contracts a day. Each combines a layout, a step mix, a hive plan and one modifier (night ops, evac frenzy, no molotovs, brute hunt), and pays research cores plus a chest.

  3. 7-day Supply Drop streak 2–3 days

    Login rewards delivered to the Outpost helipad, with a weapon camo on day 7.

  4. Hive Siege endless mode ~2 weeks

    Hives respawn in tiers on cleared layouts. Extract after wave 5 to bank your loot. A weekly seed feeds a Play Games leaderboard.

  5. Mastery stars and the missing arsenal ~3 weeks

    Three stars per contract (extract, find all intel, beat a time or damage target) give a reason to replay all 13. Ship the shotgun, the SMG and the remaining skills.

After those, in order: a weekly Alpha Brute event, opt-in notifications, cloud save and achievements, Outpost offline income, then a 30-day pass once dailies and endless mode exist.

Make it explosive

  • Game feel: add a brief freeze and slow-motion when a hive bursts or the boss dies. Show the chopper landing in slow motion for the last 5 seconds of an evac. Add kill-streak callouts, and zoom the camera in or make characters 20–25% larger.
  • Own the look: keep the stark white world and use crimson only for threats and blood. That contrast is the store page and the trailer.
  • Moments worth sharing: chained hive bursts, last-second evacs and flashlight night ops. Add a share card after each run with kills, hives and extraction time.
  • Store listing:
    • Title: "Protocol Crimson: Zombie Ops".
    • Short description: "Burn the hives, hold the LZ, extract alive."
    • A full-bleed icon that matches real gameplay, and check you have the rights to the current art.
    • A 1024×500 feature graphic and six captioned 1080×1920 screenshots.

Monetization

Free-to-play with a premium feel, tunable through remote config:

  • Rewarded ads for double loot, recovering full loot after death, one revive per run and a daily drop.
  • A $3.99 Supporter pack and a starter pack.
  • Cosmetics: skins, camos, tracer colours, and blood and fire colours.
  • A 30-day pass once live content exists.

Guardrails:

  • No energy timers and no loot boxes.
  • No ads during combat and no forced interstitials at launch.
  • At most one paid revive per run.
  • Every gameplay item can be earned.
  • 13+ audience, with Google's consent tool (UMP) for EU and UK players.

Roadmap

Weeks 1–3 · before soft launch

Readiness sprint

  • Week 1: AAB pipeline, private key, API 36, version-code bumps, exclude dev files, back button and auto-pause, intel fix, analytics and crash reporting, privacy policy.
  • Week 2: tutorial; rebalance so an idle player loses 15–35% HP per sector-1 contract; global cap on hive defenders; game-feel effects.
  • Week 3: Daily Ops, login streak, mastery stars, object pooling, performance pass on real phones, store assets, closed test with 20+ testers.
6–8 weeks · soft launch

Prove retention

  • Hive Siege and leaderboards.
  • Shotgun, SMG and the remaining skills.
  • Weekly boss event, notifications and cloud save.
  • Rewarded ads and packs.
  • A/B tests on stick side, auto-fire range and camera distance.

  • 30-day pass and localization.
  • Offline income and pre-registration.

Strengths to double down on

  • The loop: hives, then varied steps, then a tense evac where stepping out of the LZ pauses the countdown.
  • Contracts as data: daily and event content is mostly configuration.
  • Replay variety: hive placement is randomized each run.
  • A fair economy: the vault is never penalized, and every payout shown is paid.
  • Engineering: atomic saves, working multi-touch and automated QA bots that play every contract.
  • A habit loop: the debrief's "Upgrade now" leads straight into the next run.

How this was measured:

  • Automated play-tests: a contract bot played all 13 contracts end to end with 0 failures. It teleports to objectives, so it doesn't test difficulty.
  • Idle and hive-fight damage probes.
  • A two-finger touch test that passed 10 of 10 checks.
  • Screenshot review, plus inspection of the APK manifest and the code.

Not yet verified: on-device performance, audio quality on a phone, and safe-area cutouts.
